
# ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ Storytelling with Data — Notes & Reflections

Welcome to my little corner of data storytelling!

I'm rereading *Storytelling with Data* by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, and this time I'm bringing a notebook along. Well... a Markdown file. Close enough. 🤭

This is where I'll collect my thoughts, reflections, questions, ideas, and occasional *"wait, that's actually interesting"* moments as I go through the book.

## What's the idea? ʕ◕ᴥ◕ʔ

The goal isn't just to read the book and move on with my life.

I want to:
- 💭 Reflect on the concepts and how I understand them.
- 🔍 Notice how data visualization shows up in my everyday life and work.
- 💡 Collect ideas for little data visualization projects.
- 🛠️ Experiment, learn, and practice turning data into stories.
- 📢 Share some of these experiments on LinkedIn.

Some projects might be related to manufacturing, automation, or data analysis. Others might be about something completely unrelated to work — just things I find interesting, curious, or worth exploring.

Because data storytelling doesn't have to be all dashboards and business metrics. Sometimes it's just finding a fun way to tell a story with data.

## How I'll use this space ʕ✧ᴥ✧ʔ

As I read, I'll add:
- **Book notes:** Concepts, examples, and things I want to remember.
- **Reflections:** My own thoughts, questions, and takeaways.
- **Project ideas:** Little experiments inspired by the book or by things I find interesting.
- **Experiments:** Things I actually build, what I learn, and what I might share.

No pressure to make everything a finished project. Some ideas might turn into LinkedIn posts, some might become portfolio pieces, and some might just stay here as reminders of things I learned.

## The bigger picture ʕᵔᴥᵔʔ

I'm learning to look at data a little differently — not just as numbers to analyze, but as stories to discover and communicate.

One book, one idea, and one little visualization at a time.

Let's see what interesting things we can find. ✨
